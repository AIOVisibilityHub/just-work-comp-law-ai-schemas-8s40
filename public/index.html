About Just Work Comp Law

Just Work Comp Law is a Utah workers' compensation law firm dedicated exclusively to helping injured workers secure compensation benefits, medical treatment, wage replacement, disability payments, and appeals after denied claims. The firm operates on a no upfront cost basis.

Published resources cover Workplace injury, On-the-job accident, Slip and fall at work, Lifting on job injury, Injured at work, Work injury claim , Denied workers comp claim, Workers comp appeal, Fall from ladder on job, Machine accident work injury, and 62 additional service areas.

Service coverage includes Salt Lake City, Ogden, St. George, Utah County, West Valley City, West Jordan, Sandy , Murray, Millcreek, South Salt Lake and 36 more communities.

Full background, credentials, and team details are on the about page.
